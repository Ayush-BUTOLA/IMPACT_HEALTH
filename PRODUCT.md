# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React (Vite) + Tailwind CSS / Vanilla CSS frontend with Java Spring Boot backend.

## Users

Hybrid audience:
- **Patients & Families**: Individuals seeking high-quality disease management, virtual/in-clinic doctor consultations, home blood tests, radiology, and medicine delivery.
- **Enterprise & B2B Partners**: Pharmaceutical companies (Patient Support Programs), Corporate organizations (Employee Health), Educational institutions (School Health), and Healthcare Practitioners.

## Product Purpose

Impact Health provides tailored, patient-first medical infrastructure and services across 200+ cities in India, serving over 200,000+ patients. The platform unifies doorstep healthcare services, chronic disease management, and specialized B2B healthcare infrastructure.

## Positioning

Patient-first medical infrastructure connecting patients, doctors, lab networks, and pharma support programs in a single unified platform.

## Operating Context

- **Patients**: Fast access to consultations, home lab test bookings, medicine orders, and chronic care management.
- **B2B / Partners**: Turnkey patient support programs, corporate wellness programs, and practitioner tools.
- **Care Team**: Central inbox dispatch (`connect@impacthealth.co.in`), phone support (`+91 9667835909`), and admin CMS management.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities**: Hero slideshow carousel, interactive service cards, leadership and advisors showcase, client marquee (Zomato, Blinkit, Bistro, etc.), integrated contact dispatch, privacy & compliance portal, and CMS admin control.
- **Constraints**: All support inquiries route via central care team (`connect@impacthealth.co.in` & `+91 9667835909`). Navigation links route strictly within the project.

## Product Principles

1. **Patient-First Simplicity**: Make healthcare access friction-free with clear pathways to consultations, diagnostics, and support.
2. **Visual Authority & Trust**: Use clean typography, crisp healthcare imagery, precise spatial rhythm, and premium UI aesthetics.
3. **Internal Integrity**: Ensure all navigation and interactive CTAs connect cleanly within the application without external broken hooks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
